Hi, I’m Ethan. I’ve always known I wanted to do something great and make a real difference, and I’ve started to back that up.

I’m a founder. I grew Kovrio, a free AP study app, to over 70 users, and I’ve now shifted my focus to Rilo. Rilo is a screen-time buddy that actually connects with you, rewards you for staying off your phone, and gives you ideas for what to do instead.

I’m a content creator. I grew my Instagram to over 4,000 followers and over 400,000 views in just two months.

I’m a builder who grinds Claude Code sessions until I fall asleep. I’m always building something: a new project, a new app, a new tool.

I’m a student. As a freshman, I’m taking three AP classes and a dual-credit business class, including a senior-level math class, three years ahead.

I’m also an athlete. I’ve played basketball, baseball, golf, and football, and right now I’m focused on basketball and golf. I’ve been playing basketball for over a decade and recently got into golf.

I know that if I really want to make a difference, I can’t do it alone. Jesus is more important than anything to me. My favorite Bible verse is Philippians 4:13: “I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.”
