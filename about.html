Who is Ethan Dunn?

I'm a 15-year-old entrepreneur, content creator, and software builder. I've always been gifted — always in the higher-up math classes, always getting the highest scores in school, always talented in sports — but at some point I realized the real potential of what I could do with those gifts. My true passion is pursuing entrepreneurship. I want to do something great, really make an impact, and I feel entrepreneurship is how I can do that.

I knew I was made to be an entrepreneur ever since 5th grade, when I was making money selling candy to my classmates. In the past couple months I really ramped things up: I built my first MVP of an app, deployed my first real app, and publicly launched my first app — three different apps, taken to three different lengths. The one I launched is live right now. It's called AllAP, and it helps students taking AP classes study and stay on top of their education. Instead of opening a textbook, they can go to AllAP and take practice quizzes on any unit or lesson they want.

I believe anyone who's unsure about what career or path to follow should try entrepreneurship. It lets you be free, creative, and truly make an impact.
