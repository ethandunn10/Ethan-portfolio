Project

Kovrio

Free AP practice quizzes. I grew it to 71 users, then shut it down in Nov 2026 to focus on Rilo.

  • 71 users
  • 9 AP subjects
  • Shut down Nov 2026
See the code (opens in a new tab)

The problem

I got tired of photographing textbook pages, uploading them to ChatGPT, and asking it to make a quiz, over and over, every unit.

What I built

Free AP practice quizzes, sorted by unit and topic, so you can drill one piece of the course at a time.

9 AP subjects

6,770 questions across 70 units and 677 topics, following the College Board’s units.

Mastery per topic and unit

A 78% average can hide a unit you don’t know. Kovrio shows the split.

Missed questions come back

Questions you miss get weighted back into your next unit quiz.

What to study next

Insights turns your scores into a study plan.

How I built it

  • HTML, CSS & JavaScript
  • Supabase
  • Google sign-in
  • Vercel
  • Remotion

What I learned

Build what you care about.

Kovrio got 71 students using it, and I’m proud of that. But I didn’t love the problem, and there were already big study tools that did it well. When you don’t care, it shows, and growth stalls.

Shipping beats perfect.

Kovrio was my first real full app: a live site, accounts, Google sign-in and a database. Getting 71 real people to use it taught me more than any tutorial.

Know when to stop.

Shutting Kovrio down wasn’t failing. It freed up my time for Rilo, something I actually care about. Quitting the right thing is a skill too.
