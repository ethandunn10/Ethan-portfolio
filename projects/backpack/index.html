Project

Backpack

An AI study buddy that turns homework into study plans and quizzes.

  • 5th place · NXT Build Sprint
  • Built for a friend
  • Nov 13–15, 2026
Watch the demo (opens in a new tab)See the code (opens in a new tab)

The problem

My friend couldn’t keep track of homework, studying, and time.

What I built

A study buddy that remembers your homework. Snap a worksheet or type what your teacher said, and it tells you what to do tonight and quizzes you on your own homework.

Snap your homework

AI pulls out what’s due, when, and what it covers.

Tonight’s plan

What to do tonight, what’s coming up, and a countdown to the next test.

A plan for every test

A day-by-day study plan up to test day.

Quizzes from your homework

Every answer shows which homework it came from.

How I built it

  • HTML, CSS & JavaScript
  • Vercel serverless function
  • Claude API

Results

place at NXT Build Sprint
5th

What I learned

  • Keep the AI key on the server and give it only two jobs, reading homework and making quizzes, so nobody can use it as a free chatbot.
  • A public AI demo needs guardrails: a rate limit per visitor, size caps on photos, short answers, a cheap model, and a low spend limit.
