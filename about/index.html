Who is Ethan Dunn?

Hi, I’m Ethan. I’m 15, I’m a freshman, and I like building things people actually use.

I’m a founder. I grew Kovrio, a free AP study app, to 71 users with no ads and no budget, then shut it down in November 2026 to focus on Rilo, an iPhone screen-time buddy: a panda that connects with you, rewards you for staying off your phone, and gives you ideas for what to do instead.

I’m a builder who grinds Claude Code sessions until I fall asleep. I’m always working on something: a new app, a new tool, a new idea. At my first hackathon, my project AirWrite took second place.

I’m a content creator. My Instagram has 5.5K+ followers and 500K+ views in the past month, and my top reel has 160K+ views. I also post long-form videos on YouTube. I also make UGC for Whop, which is how I made my first dollar online.

I’m a student. I’m taking AP Bio, AP US Gov, and AP Precalc, plus a dual-credit Intro to Business class, and I have all A’s. I’m in FBLA and a member of KY Combinator.

I’m an athlete, too. I’ve played basketball for over a decade, and I recently got into golf.

Jesus is more important to me than anything. My favorite verse is Philippians 4:13: “I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.”

If you’re working on something interesting, or you think we should build something together, I’d like to hear about it.
