04 Software · AI 2nd at Hack the Track

AirWrite

Write on a blank sheet of paper with your finger and get a real PDF. Built at my first hackathon, where it placed runner-up.

Role
Builder
Built
Sep 2026
Result
Runner-up
How it works

An overhead camera tracks your fingertip and maps it onto a virtual page; a side camera watches how high your finger is to tell when the “pen” is down. Your strokes export as a true vector PDF, or Claude turns them into typed text. Everything runs in the browser.
